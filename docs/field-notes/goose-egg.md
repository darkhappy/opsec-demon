# Soul escrow account

Fictional filing authority: NIGHTFALL / ABYSS-999.

`egg start`, `egg status`, and `egg hatch` manage the simulated Golden Goose Egg. The session wallet starts at 2,400 souls; an egg costs 800.

The egg carries 400 initial souls and earns 80 per real minute. Initial souls grant no buffs. Buffs and balances are simulation state.
