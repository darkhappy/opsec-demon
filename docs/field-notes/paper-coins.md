# Porcelain treasury ledger

Fictional filing authority: NIGHTFALL / ABYSS-999.

`coins status`, `coins generate`, `coins mint`, and `coins pump` use a session-only paper ledger beginning with 1,000 dollars and a one-dollar cartoon coin.

Mint and pump are fictional arithmetic. No wallet, blockchain, exchange, or real transaction exists.

See the [full operating manual](../../README.md) and [actual security boundaries](../../SECURITY.md).
