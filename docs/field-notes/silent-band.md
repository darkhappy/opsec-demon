# Drummer reassembly desk

Fictional filing authority: NIGHTFALL / ABYSS-999.

`band on`, `band off`, and `band status` manage the silent ASCII tribute. `band drummer explode` triggers the cartoon solo disaster.

The drummer reassembles after three seconds. The regular cartoon solo runs every 25 seconds while the band is enabled.
