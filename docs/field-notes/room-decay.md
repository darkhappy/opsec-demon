# Sabine chamber worksheet

Fictional filing authority: NIGHTFALL / ABYSS-999.

`rt60 set 5 4 3 0.2` supplies width, length, height in meters and mean absorption. `rt60 status` prints the estimate; `graph room` selects its decay curve.

Sabine RT60 is 0.161 times volume divided by surface area times absorption. This is a single broadband idealized model without microphones.
