# Lost Signals / original terminal visual novel

An original non-explicit fan tribute prompted by Tama-soft's Lost Child: new setting,
characters, dialogue, and music. This repository includes no original game's script,
art, executable, or soundtrack. The original product site was unavailable during this
work, so the tribute makes no claims of canon fidelity or an official adaptation.

```text
novel start
novel next
novel choose 1
novel next
psyche
mascot tweeter
mascot krillion
graph novel
```

The story has ten shared scenes, two choice gates, and two endings. At scenes four and
seven, choose `1` or `2` before continuing. Two listening choices reach the Open Channel;
other combinations reach the Guarded Channel. `novel reset` clears the route.
There are 2,251 words across both endings and roughly 2,000 words on a complete route.
The full original text lives in `assets/story/lost-signals.md` and is embedded in the binary.

The right panel shows an excerpt; PgUp/PgDn scroll the complete scene in the transcript.
Ctrl-F cycles the novel tab alongside metrics and six synthetic graphs. `psyche` describes
the fictional protagonist's coping metaphors and choices; it does not profile the user,
collect personal information, or diagnose a mental health condition.
